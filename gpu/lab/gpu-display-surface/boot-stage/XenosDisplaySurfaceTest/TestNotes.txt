Xenos WindowServer display surface diagnostic

The gpudisplaysurface profile loads XenonCoreGraphics 0.0.5d42.
Over SSH, use /bin/sh RunXenosDisplaySurface.sh --probe from this
directory after cold boot. The probe uses Tiger's IOAccelFindAccelerator
on the active Xbox framebuffer, then creates a 32x32 surface through
IOAccelCreateSurface. Two native flush calls clear the offscreen surface
to different colors. Each IOAccelReadLockSurface returns a read-only
linear pixel page. The probe checks all 1,024 pixels per flush, verifies
a fresh GPU ticket and fence owned by its PID, unlocks and destroys
the surface. It also checks that a malformed shape and a flush while
locked are rejected. On 0.0.5d41, --baseline verifies that the native
surface open is unavailable.

The driver accepts a reserved diagnostic surface ID and keeps AccelCaps
at zero. The surface is offscreen and controlled by the SSH probe.
Normal windows and Quartz Extreme still use the software compositor.
